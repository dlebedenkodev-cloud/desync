DeSync — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 September 2026 · Last updated: 7 September 2026

1. Who we are

DeSync (“the game”) is an iOS puzzle game published by Denys Lebedenko, an individual developer (“we”, “us”). For questions about this policy or about your data, write to dlebedenko.play@gmail.com.

This policy explains what the game does with data. It covers the iOS app only. It does not cover the App Store itself, which is operated by Apple.

2. Short version

  • The game has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or address.
  • Your progress stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. Ads are the only reason any identifier leaves your device.
  • We collect anonymous gameplay statistics (which level people quit on) and crash reports.
  • In the EEA, the UK and regulated US states you are asked for consent before any of that starts, and you can change your answer at any time from the game’s settings.

3. Data that never leaves your device

The following is stored locally (iOS UserDefaults) and is not transmitted to us:

  • campaign progress and stars per level;
  • bonus hints you have earned;
  • how many times in a row you have restarted the current level (used to hand you a free hint when you are stuck);
  • whether the game has already asked you to rate it;
  • your chosen language and the music/sound switches.

Progress and earned hints are also mirrored to Apple’s iCloud key-value storage so that they survive a reinstall or a move to a new device. That copy lives in your iCloud account. We have no access to it. You can remove it in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

The game shows full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded ads in exchange for an extra hint. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC), acting as an independent controller of the data it collects.

AdMob may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) when you allow tracking;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address (country/region level — the game never requests GPS location);
  • information about ads shown, clicked or skipped, and technical diagnostics;
  • device model, operating system and app version.

If you allow tracking and consent to personalised ads, this data may be used to select ads based on your interests. If you refuse, the game still shows ads, but non-personalised ones; a limited amount of data is still processed for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement, which is what makes ad delivery possible at all.

How Google uses this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

SKAdNetwork

The game supports Apple’s SKAdNetwork, a privacy-preserving install attribution system. It reports to ad networks that an install happened, without any identifier that points at you.

5. Consent — Google User Messaging Platform

In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and regulated US states, the game shows a consent form on first launch, before the advertising SDK is started at all. Your answer is stored on your device by Google’s User Messaging Platform.

You can change that answer at any time: open the game, tap Settings → Privacy settings. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it, and it takes effect immediately.

6. App Tracking Transparency

On iOS the game asks the system tracking permission (“Allow DeSync to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites?”). Refusing costs you nothing: the game works identically, and you keep getting ads, just non-personalised ones. You can change your answer in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

7. Gameplay statistics — Firebase Analytics

A sixty-level campaign is only as good as its difficulty curve, and the one question the design cannot answer on its own is where players give up. We use Google Firebase Analytics to answer it. The events sent are:

EventWhat it carries
level startedlevel number
level completedlevel number, stars, moves used, par, seconds spent
level restartedlevel number, how many attempts in a row
level abandonedlevel number, moves used, seconds spent
hint usedlevel number, hints remaining
rewarded ad finishedlevel number, whether it was watched to the end

Firebase adds its own automatic parameters: a random app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, language and country. None of this identifies you by name, and the game has no accounts to link it to.

These events are sent only after you have consented. Because we pass the same consent on to Google for advertising purposes as well, this data may also be used for ad measurement and personalisation — which is why the game declares app-measurement.com as a tracking domain, and why iOS blocks it outright if you deny tracking.

8. Crash reports — Firebase Crashlytics

When the game crashes, a report is sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics containing the crash stack, device model, operating system version, app version, a randomly generated installation identifier and the number of the level being played at the time.

Crash reports are collected regardless of your advertising choice, on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the game from crashing. They are not linked to your identity and are never used for advertising.

9. Purchases

This version of the game has no in-app purchases. There is nothing to buy, no payment information is ever requested, and the game contains no payment screen of any kind.

If a purchase is added in a later version, the transaction will be handled entirely by Apple: we would never see your payment details, and the only thing reaching the game would be a yes/no entitlement. This page is updated before any such change reaches players — see section 16.

10. Ratings

The game may ask you to rate it using Apple’s standard in-app review prompt. Anything you write there goes to Apple, not to us, and we cannot tell whether you left a review.

11. What we never collect

No name, email, phone number, postal address or date of birth. No precise location. No contacts, photos, calendar, health data, microphone or camera access. No reading of other apps or files. The game requests none of these permissions, and the code contains no way to use them.

12. Children

DeSync is not directed at children and is not listed in the App Store Kids Category. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a child has provided such data, write to us and we will have it deleted.

13. Where data goes and how long it is kept

Google processes the data described above on servers in the United States and other countries, under the safeguards set out in its own privacy policy, including the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

  • Analytics events — retained for the period configured in our Firebase project; Google offers retention windows between 2 and 14 months.
  • Crash reports — retained by Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Advertising data — retained by Google under its own policies.
  • On-device data — kept until you reset progress in the game’s settings or delete the app.

14. Your rights

If you are in the EEA or the UK, the GDPR gives you the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

The practical ways to exercise these rights:

  • Withdraw or change consent — Settings → Privacy settings, inside the game.
  • Stop tracking — iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Reset the advertising identifier — iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (turning it off resets it).
  • Erase local data — Settings → Reset progress, or delete the app.
  • Erase the iCloud copy — iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.
  • Everything else — write to dlebedenko.play@gmail.com.
Please note: the data we receive carries no identifier that would let us find “you” in it. To act on an erasure request for analytics or crash data we may need the app-instance identifier, which we cannot obtain on our own. Deleting the app and resetting your advertising identifier is the fastest and most complete way to stop all of it.

15. California and other US states

We do not sell personal information for money. Sharing data for cross-context behavioural advertising happens only where you have consented or not opted out. To opt out, refuse the tracking prompt or change your answer in the game’s privacy settings — that is our recognised opt-out mechanism for the CCPA/CPRA and equivalent state laws.

16. Changes to this policy

If the game starts doing something materially different with data, this page is updated and the “last updated” date at the top changes with it. The current version is always the one linked from the game’s settings and from the App Store listing.
